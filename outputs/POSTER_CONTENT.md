# Poster copy: The Uncounted Economy
## Water, firewood, cattle: work that GDP does not see

### Hero finding

## 6 h 10 m
**Average time per day rural women aged 25-44 spend on own-use goods production and unpaid domestic services.**

**98.74%** participate. Weighted numerator/denominator: **123.65 million / 125.22 million people**. Diary sample: **53,144** (52,441 participants).

Same age and place, men average **53.6 minutes/day**; **44.28%** participate. The descriptive time gap is **5 h 17 m/day**.

### What makes up the day?

| Rural women, age 25-44 | Minutes/person/day |
|---|---:|
| Unpaid domestic services (ICATUS division 3) | 337.1 |
| Own-use goods production (division 2) | 33.3 |
| **Combined** | **370.3** |

This is not only a participation gap: the division-3 domestic-service mean drives most of the difference.

### Four-group comparison

Weighted mean minutes per person per day, with nonparticipants counted as zero:

| Residence and gender | Participation in divisions 2+3 | Mean time/person/day |
|---|---:|---:|
| Rural women | 82.70% | 266.2 min (4 h 26 m) |
| Rural men | 37.24% | 47.9 min (48 m) |
| Urban women | 81.14% | 236.1 min (3 h 56 m) |
| Urban men | 30.16% | 27.7 min (28 m) |

Rural women who participate average **321.9 minutes/day (5 h 22 m)**; that participant-conditioned mean is different from the 266.2-minute all-person mean.

### Resource work

- **Water:** 11.08% of rural women participate in collection (weighted `37.70m / 340.23m`; `n=142,080`). Participants average **41.7 minutes/day**. Rural men's rate is 3.57%.
- **Firewood:** 18.64% of households using firewood/chips for cooking have a diary member collect firewood, versus 1.26% of households using other recorded cooking fuels (`n=36,690` vs `102,793`; household-weighted rates).
- **Animal farming:** 7.13% of all valid age-6+ diary people participate; animal-farming participants average **105.6 minutes/day**.

### Geography

- **Highest combined participation:** Nagaland, **78.79%** (`n=3,420`); weighted numerator/denominator `845,737 / 1,073,473`.
- **Highest all-person mean:** Arunachal Pradesh, **234.6 minutes/day** (`n=3,962`).
- **Largest measured state gender gap:** Arunachal Pradesh women **396.6 minutes/day** vs men **84.5**; gap **5 h 12 m**. Unweighted samples: 1,898 women and 2,062 men.

### Method/footer copy

**Source:** MoSPI/NSSO Time Use Survey 2024, official microdata and documentation. Weighted estimates use the final multiplier `MULT/100`. Time in shared activity intervals is allocated equally across activities, following the official README's allowed treatment. Person estimates use 450,452 valid age-6+ diaries; household rates use households with at least one valid diary member. Division 4 caregiving is excluded. These are weighted descriptive estimates; survey-design standard errors were not calculated. Household and state associations are not causal findings.
